- New cobalt accent replaces wine across the site for a sharper, more fashion-forward identity.
- Homepage gains a stronger editorial journal layer and service/value band.
- Hero gets stronger navigation cues and campaign framing.
- Campaign imagery remains clearly labeled as demo placeholder imagery.
