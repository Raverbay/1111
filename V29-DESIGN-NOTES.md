# Flip&Co V29 — World Class Fashion Retail

V29 is a full visual/art-direction pass based on research of leading luxury fashion e-commerce patterns. It is an original implementation, not a pixel-copy of any third-party site.

## Direction
- Editorial luxury + contemporary streetwear
- Bone / espresso / wine palette
- Large typography, controlled whitespace, image-led merchandising
- Full-screen hero showcase with rotating product stories
- Preloader / brand entrance
- Curated product rail rather than generic catalog-first homepage
- Brand marquee and dark editorial interlude
- Finder → personalized edit
- Store / human styling service as a core differentiator

## Research references
- LuisaViaRoma: category-led merchandising, top-brand navigation and phygital positioning.
- Mytheresa: curated luxury edit and strong product/service presentation.
- Browns: editorial luxury retail and immersive store/commerce experience.
- Ecommerce Design Awards: fashion references including Hermès, Loro Piana, The Row and other custom fashion builds.
